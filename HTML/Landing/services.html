Ganal-Mappala Dental Clinic Appointment System

Patient Agreement

Please read these terms carefully before creating an account and using the dental appointment system.

1. Account Registration

By registering, you confirm that the information you provide is accurate, complete, and up to date. You are responsible for maintaining the confidentiality of your login credentials.

2. Appointment Requests

Submitting an appointment request through the system does not automatically guarantee final confirmation. The clinic may review, reschedule, or adjust appointments based on availability and clinical considerations.

3. Patient Responsibility

Patients are expected to provide truthful personal and contact information, arrive on time, and follow clinic instructions before and after treatment.

Lateness Policy

If you arrive 30 minutes or more after your scheduled appointment time, your appointment will be automatically cancelled by clinic staff.

If you have already made a payment, it will be fully refunded to you.

Repeated no-shows or misuse of the system may result in account restrictions.

4. Fees, Payments, and Refund Policy

Treatment fees shown in the system are subject to clinic review and may vary depending on the final dental assessment. Required down payments, when applicable, must be completed according to clinic policy.

Refund Options

If YOU cancel your appointment: You may choose your preferred refund method — either Cash or GCash.

If the CLINIC/Dentist cancels your appointment: You will also be offered the choice of refund via Cash or GCash.

5. Privacy and Data Use

Your personal information is collected only for account management, appointment scheduling, and clinic communication purposes. Your data will be kept confidential and will not be shared with unauthorized parties.

6. System Availability

While we aim to keep the appointment system available and accurate, temporary interruptions, maintenance, or technical issues may occur. The clinic is not liable for delays caused by system downtime beyond reasonable control.

7. Acceptance of Terms

By checking the agreement box and registering, you acknowledge that you have read, understood, and agreed to these Terms and Conditions for use of the Ganal-Mappala Dental Clinic Appointment System.
